# Managing Feedback & Notes

Review cycles and supervisor feedback are an essential part of production. OpenStudio Hub brings this communication directly to your desktop, keeping you in the zone.

## The Integrated Activity Feed
Instead of constantly switching between Blender and your web browser to check Kitsu for updates, you can manage your feedback directly inside the Hub.

* **Centralized Inbox:** The Hub features an integrated Activity Feed that displays all notes, retakes, and approvals assigned to your tasks.
* **Acknowledge Notes:** When you read a correction note from your supervisor, you can mark it as read by using the "Acknowledge" action directly from the Hub's interface. This instantly syncs with the Kitsu database, letting your supervisor know you have seen the feedback.

By keeping communication inside the Hub, you minimize distractions and maintain a seamless, focused workflow from your first draft to the final approved shot.
